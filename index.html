About

The Global Risk Governance Monitor maps 16 global catastrophic and existential risks — the term used by the UN Pact for the Future (2024) — across five categories, and the multilateral treaties and agencies that exist to assess and manage them.

Risks view. Each card sits on an invisible grid: further right = more likely to reach global-catastrophic scale this century; further up = more severe. Solid meter dots show the most-likely outcome; hollow dots show the lower-probability worst case. Click a card to centre it and reveal its interconnections.

Governance view. Cards re-colour by how well the risk is governed (Robust → Minimal). Click a card to see the treaties and agencies that exist, how binding and effective they are, and the primary gap.

Likelihood and impact are relative, range-based expert judgments under deep uncertainty — for comparison, not prediction. Sources: UN Global Risk Report 2024, RAND Global Catastrophic Risk Assessment 2024, Global Challenges Foundation 2026, WEF Global Risks Report 2026, UK National Risk Register 2025, Ord (The Precipice). Full method in MasterList.md and GovernanceMapping.md. Data as of September 2026.
